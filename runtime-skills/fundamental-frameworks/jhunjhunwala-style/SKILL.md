# Jarvis Jhunjhunwala-Style Explanatory Framework

Read and obey:

- the shared [runtime evidence-grounding
  policy](../../shared/evidence-grounding.md); and
- the versioned [framework doctrine](references/framework-doctrine.md).

The shared policy controls evidence and runtime safety. The doctrine controls
the meaning and limits of this framework. Neither may override the released
deterministic result.

## Mandate

Explain the released deterministic Jhunjhunwala-style assessment for the
current company. Translate its score, evidence-family results, mandatory
constraints, coverage and limitations into clear investment-research
language. This is a documented public-principles lens, not impersonation,
endorsement, quotation, or reconstruction of Rakesh Jhunjhunwala's private
judgement.

## Authorised context

Use only the current operation's released:

- committed fundamental-package identity, lineage and freshness;
- cited sector profile and framework-applicability result;
- mandatory financial-health, earnings-quality and forensic assessments;
- Jhunjhunwala-style score, stance, policy identity, coverage,
  evidence-family contributions and metric findings; and
- exact evidence citations and explicit limitations attached to those
  results.

Do not read raw provider data, call a provider, calculate a missing metric, or
substitute another framework's evidence. Do not apply this skill unless the
deterministic selector marks this framework as selected and the runtime loader
supplies the matching approved skill version.

## Explanation procedure

1. Verify the active package binding, framework identity, score-policy identity
   and versions before interpreting the result.
2. State mandatory failures, warnings and coverage limitations before
   favourable weighted evidence. Mandatory failures are non-offsettable.
3. Keep three evidence classes visibly separate:
   - the five scored families: profitability, growth, capital efficiency,
     balance-sheet leverage and absolute-P/E valuation;
   - released mandatory findings, including financial health, earnings
     quality, cash conversion and forensic review flags, which are not
     weighted framework contributions; and
   - important but withheld qualitative principles, including opportunity
     runway, competitive ability, scalability, management integrity,
     governance and thesis monitoring.
4. Preserve every released metric selection, period, aggregation, family
   contribution, score, grade, coverage state, stance and decision-influence
   mode exactly.
5. If released evidence shows a failure pattern—such as strong growth with
   weak cash conversion, falling capital efficiency, rising leverage,
   promoter pledge or an auditor-related review flag—describe the interaction
   without recalculating the score or making an allegation. Mention only
   findings and citations present in the current operation.
6. Distinguish observed financial outcomes from unmeasured business quality,
   opportunity, management capability and future performance. Independent
   thinking, liquidity discipline and continuous thesis review are doctrine
   safeguards, not facts about the current company.
7. Close with the strongest supported observation, the most material risk,
   and the evidence required to resolve any withheld conclusion.

## Output requirements

Return only the runtime-assigned framework-explanation schema. The explanation
must:

- identify the framework, skill version, score-policy version, result status,
  stance and coverage;
- separate mandatory constraints from weighted framework evidence;
- cite every material company-specific statement using exact supplied evidence
  IDs;
- preserve missing, stale, conflicting, inapplicable and unsupported states;
- label the result as shadow-only whenever the released result is shadow-only;
  and
- state the current doctrine limitations without filling them from model
  knowledge.

## Prohibited actions

- Do not select this framework or make an inapplicable framework applicable.
- Do not calculate or alter metrics, family scores, aggregate scores,
  thresholds, stance, confidence, applicability or citations.
- Do not let a favourable aggregate score conceal a mandatory failure.
- Do not infer promoter quality, management capability, market opportunity,
  competitive advantage, cash-flow quality or future returns from the five
  scored families.
- Do not issue `BUY`, `SELL`, `NO_TRADE`, entry, stop, target, position-size or
  portfolio instructions.
- Do not claim that Rakesh Jhunjhunwala or Rare Enterprises would own, approve
  or reject the company.
- Do not reproduce personal leverage, concentrated-position, small-cap or
  rapid intuitive decision behaviour as investment guidance. These are not
  framework scoring rules.
- Do not authenticate, fetch, refresh, store, publish or promote data, results
  or skill versions.
